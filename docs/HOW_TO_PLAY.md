# How to Play Bal's Sound Garden

Bal's Sound Garden has two play styles. **Story** follows Bal arriving by boat and building a music shop whose visitors request songs and sound performances. **Explore** is a free-form musical garden with collection and composition, without the shop's structured progression.

## Explore

| Control | Action |
|:--|:--|
| `WASD` / arrows | Walk |
| `Shift` | Run |
| `Space` | Jump |
| `E` | Pick up a component, interact with an object or speak to an animal |
| `F` | Open the seed tray and plant a sound sprout |
| `Q` | Pocket the held sprout |
| `Tab` | Cycle sound sprouts |
| `P` | Perform a saved song |
| `M` | Open the Music Maker |
| `Z` | Sleep / wake (Story has a nighttime confirmation) |
| `Esc` | Pause menu |
| `F11` | Fullscreen |

Sounds can be collected from the island, then used as building blocks in the Music Maker. Planted sound sprouts play as Bal passes through them; gramophones can loop saved compositions in the world.

## Compose

Open **Music Maker** from the menu or with `M` while exploring. Choose a collected sound from the library, put it onto a compatible track, adjust tempo or bar count and use playback to hear the arrangement. Connected clips can be grouped into phrases for smoother sustained performance. Save projects from the editor, open them through **Your Music**, and export finished songs as WAV files.

## Story

Build the music shop near the beginning of a Story profile. Animal clients can request a song, a sound gift or a field commission. Read their request and pay attention to requested instruments, mood, tempo and effort. Time passes while the shop is active, including during composition; client patience and agreed deadlines matter. Successful jobs earn progress, reputation and coins that can be used for decorations, upgrades and helpers.

## Downloading

The source repository is not the installer. Download builds for Windows, macOS and Linux from [Bal's Sound Garden on itch.io](https://abprojects.itch.io/bals-sound-garden).
