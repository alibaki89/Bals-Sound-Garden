The MIT license in this repository applies to original source code, **not automatically to game artwork or other creative assets**.

Unless an individual asset is explicitly accompanied by its own license or documented permission, **no additional public reuse license is granted by this repository** for:

- Sprites, portraits, animations, UI artwork, background scenes, image assets, and menu animation frames.
- Recorded sound effects, audio samples, compositions, soundtrack elements, and other prerecorded media. This distinction does not prevent reuse of MIT-licensed software routines used to generate sound.
- Literary content, dialogue, character designs, original naming, and trademark-like branding.
- Screenshots from the official itch.io page, the GitHub Pages website, and other project presentations.

Viewers are welcome to visit the project's [official itch.io page](https://abprojects.itch.io/bals-sound-garden). Downloading the game and browsing its public source do not by themselves grant an independent right to redistribute its creative assets.

**Asset provenance:** The source archive includes an [`ASSET_LICENSE_REVIEW.md`](ASSET_LICENSE_REVIEW.md) checklist covering items whose provenance or redistribution rights still need explicit confirmation. The project owner must resolve these entries before making unsupported licensing or ownership claims. Content derived from third parties remains subject to its original terms.
